# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful, welcoming, and patient. People of all backgrounds and experience levels are welcome.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Focus on what's best for the project and its users.
- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, and publishing others' private information are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by contacting the maintainer through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/zeeshantanveer). All reports are reviewed and handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions, or ban contributors who
break this code, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
